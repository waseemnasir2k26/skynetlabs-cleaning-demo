06 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are you licensed and insured? ▼
Yes. Licensed in Florida, $2M general liability, $500K worker's comp. Certificates available on request — and the Google Guaranteed badge confirms it independently.
What's your service area? ▼
All of Seminole County: Lake Mary, Sanford, Oviedo, Winter Springs, Altamonte Springs, Casselberry, Longwood, Heathrow, Geneva. Bordering ZIPs case-by-case.
Do I need to be home? ▼
No. About 70% of our clients give us a code or hide-a-key. We're bonded and your team is the same crew every visit.
What products do you use? ▼
Eco-friendly, pet-safe, kid-safe by default. Branded products on request (we'll use what you have if you prefer). No ammonia, no bleach unless you ask.
What if I'm not happy? ▼
Tell us within 24 hours. We come back and re-clean the area, free. We've never had a client take us up on it more than once.
How does pricing work? ▼
Flat-rate based on bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and frequency. Recurring saves 15–25%. We send a written quote — what you see is what you pay.
